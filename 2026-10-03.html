Did You Know?

  • Eastern Rosella. Eastern rosellas are highly territorial and may destroy the eggs of other rosellas nesting too close to their own nest. [source]
  • Eastern Rosella. The eastern rosella mostly feeds on grass seeds, buds, flowers, nectars, fruits, insects and larvae. [source]
  • Yellow-rumped Thornbill. Its song is described as twittering, musical, sweet and high-pitched, and it mimics other birds, especially the alarm calls of the noisy miner. [source]
