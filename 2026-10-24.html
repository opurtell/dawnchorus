Masked Lapwing heads a 13-species day

Heard twice, among 13 species at the station in the last 24 hours.

The Masked Lapwing (Vanellus miles) was heard twice in the 24 hours to 9:30 pm, first at 1:24 am and last at 1:24 am. Its clearest call scored 95% confidence.

It has now been heard on eleven days of the station's 66. BirdNET's range model rates it one of the most widespread birds around Canberra. Atlas of Living Australia records show it lives in the Canberra region year-round.

Did You Know?

  • Masked Lapwing. Young lapwings may stay with their parents for one to two years, forming family groups of three to five birds. [source]
  • Masked Lapwing. The masked lapwing is the largest member of its family, measuring 30 to 37 cm long with a wingspan of 75–85 cm. [source]
  • Weebill. By eating scale insects, psyllids and lerps, weebills help maintain the health of trees. [source]
