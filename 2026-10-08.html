Red Wattlebird sets a new mark

Seven calls made yesterday evening the Red Wattlebird's busiest day yet at the microphone, while ten species and 176 calls filled the 24 hours to 8:30 am today.

The Red Wattlebird had its busiest day yet, with 7 calls in the window. Its previous best was 2 calls in a day. All of them came yesterday evening, between a first call at 6:53 pm and a last at 7:34 pm.

It is one of the most widespread birds around Canberra and lives in the region year-round. Over winter it gathers in larger groups of up to several hundred birds.

The station has heard it on 16 of the 50 days it has been listening. Yesterday's count stands well clear of anything before it.

The Regular

Eurasian Coot

The Eurasian Coot was heard twice, first at 5:19 pm and last at 6:31 pm yesterday. It is a noisy bird with a wide repertoire of crackling, explosive, or trumpeting calls, often given at night.

Reluctant to fly, it takes off by running across the water surface with much splashing. The station has heard it on 28 of the 50 days it has been listening.
