Big day for the Silvereye

Heard 186 times, among twelve species at the station in the last 24 hours.

The Silvereye (Zosterops lateralis) was heard 186 times in the 24 hours to 9:30 pm, first at 5:55 am and last at 7:24 pm. Its clearest call scored 99% confidence.

That beats its previous best of 159 calls in a day. It has now been heard on 36 days of the station's 59. BirdNET's range model rates it one of the most widespread birds around Canberra. Atlas of Living Australia records show it lives in the Canberra region year-round.

It joined the dawn chorus at 5:55 am, 23 minutes before sunrise.

Did You Know?

  • Silvereye. Being so small, silvereyes simply ignore bird nets, popping in and out through orchard netting at will. [source]
  • Silvereye. Silvereyes do valuable pest control in gardens and orchards, eating aphids, scale insects and the diamondback moth. [source]
  • Australian Raven. A breeding pair and their brood can hold a territory of up to around 120 hectares all year round. [source]
