Lead story

A single wheet from the Spotted Pardalote

Eleven species and 322 calls in the 24 hours to 9:30 pm today, with a lone pardalote call the middle of the day's story.

The Spotted Pardalote was heard once in the 24 hours to 9:30 pm, a single call at 1:03 pm. That was the whole of its contribution: first heard and last heard in the same minute.

It is one of the most widespread birds around Canberra and lives in the region year-round. Across the 53 days the station has been listening, it has been heard on 10 of them.

The species is moderately common across fertile parts of Australia, yet it is seldom seen closely enough to identify. One call, then, was the day's full account of a bird that keeps itself out of sight.

The Regular

Pied Currawong

The Pied Currawong was heard on 53 of the 53 days the station has been listening, and it was there again today: 30 calls between 5:50 am and 7:37 pm. Its first dawn call came at 6:00 am, 26 minutes before sunrise.

Its day stretched from two calls in the dark before dawn to its last at 7:37 pm. Pied currawongs are noisiest early in the morning and in the evening before roosting, and today's hours fit that shape.
