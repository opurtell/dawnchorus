Big day for the Pacific Koel

Heard 221 times, among twelve species at the station in the last 24 hours.

The Pacific Koel (Eudynamys orientalis) was heard 221 times in the 24 hours to 9:30 pm, first at 2:34 am and last at 6:08 pm. Its clearest call scored 98% confidence.

That beats its previous best of 44 calls in a day. It has now been heard on six days of the station's 63. BirdNET's range model rates it a moderately common bird around Canberra. Atlas of Living Australia records show it is a summer migrant to the Canberra region, most often recorded in January, November and December.

Did You Know?

  • Pacific Koel. In Australia, seeing a Pacific Koel is traditionally taken as a sign that spring and the rainy season have arrived. [source]
  • Pacific Koel. The female is brown on the back with white spots and has a cream-coloured underbelly with fine black stripes. [source]
  • Eurasian Blackbird. The male defends his territory with a "bow and run" display: a short run, then raising and bowing his head with his tail dipped. [source]
